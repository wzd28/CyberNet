## Non-negotiables
Every video must be:
1. **Super niche.** Built for one specific viewer with one specific problem, not "businesses" or "everyone".
2. **Unique.** Unlike what others in the category are doing. If a competitor could run it with their logo swapped in, it fails.
3. **Eye-catching with a wow factor.** Frame one has to earn the next second, and there must be at least one moment people would screenshot or send to a friend.
4. **Distinct.** Each video gets its own visual idea, device or format. No recycled template look across videos.
5. **Tied to its topic.** The visuals *are* the subject (the product, the service, the craft), never decoration laid over it.
6. **Built for maximum reach.** Native format per platform, a hook in the first 1–3 s, a loop or rewatch reason, and a share or save trigger.
7. **Thorough and clean.** No AI artefacts (melting hands, warped text, morphing logos), no typos, safe-zone-correct captions, clean audio, and the brand used correctly.

## Hard bans (until told otherwise)
- Stock-footage look; generic "AI business" montages (glowing brains, handshakes, holographic dashboards, people pointing at screens).
- Talking heads, unless the talking head *is* the wow factor.
- Opening on a logo, "Hey guys", or "In today's video…".
- Generic AI voiceover cadence, or royalty-free "corporate uplifting" music.
