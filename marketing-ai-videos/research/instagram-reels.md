# Instagram Reels - short-form video research (as of 2026-11-05)

Evidence note: official Instagram/Meta pages (@creators blog, Meta Transparency Center, Help Center) were read through search-result excerpts; direct page fetches were blocked in this session. Mosseri statements are mostly known via trade-press write-ups of his videos and posts. Vendor-only figures are marked "(vendor data, unverified)".

## 1. Specs

| Item | Value | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 full screen; profile grid shows a centered 3:4 crop since Jan 2025 | [3][9] |
| Resolution | 1080x1920 | [10] |
| File | MP4 (H.264, AAC) is the safe default; no official bitrate published in sources found | [10] |
| Max length | Recording/upload up to 20 min reported for many accounts in 2025-2026, rollout varies (no primary Mosseri post found) | [11] |
| Recommendation cap | Instagram (Jan 2025): Reels up to 3 min are eligible for recommendation to non-followers (previously 90 s). Reels over 3 min are generally not recommended to unconnected audiences | [1][11] |
| Ideal length | No official sweet spot. Mosseri says both relative watch time (share watched) and absolute watch time (seconds) count, so a longer Reel is not penalized if people keep watching | [2][4] |

Safe zones (1080x1920). Meta publishes ad safe zones only; nothing official for organic Reels [12].
- Meta Reels ads: keep the top 14% (about 269 px), bottom 35% (about 672 px) and 6% per side (about 65 px) free of key text/logos [12]. One 2026 report says Meta now frames the bottom as 20-35%; another advises 40% bottom for ads with disclaimers (vendor data, unverified) [12].
- Organic estimates conflict: bottom 280 px + right 90 px; or top 250 px + bottom 350 px; or a centered band y=220-1440 (vendor data, unverified) [10].
- Grid crop: 3:4 center crop of a 1080x1920 frame keeps roughly y=240-1680. Put the cover title in the center so it survives the grid and the full-screen UI.
- Working rule: key text in y=270-1250, x=65-990 (ads-safe), faces centered.

## 2. Hooks (first 1-3 s)

Mosseri (Oct 2024 video): the first three seconds of a reel are "super important", and his first suggestion was to put text on the video [5]. He gave no exact number; claims like a "1.7-second decision window" are unsourced [5].

Patterns, with examples:
- Text hook on frame 1 that states the payoff: "3 prompts that write a week of captions."
- Visual before/after split screen in the first frame.
- Send-bait framing (sends matter, see section 6): "Send this to the friend who still does invoices in Excel."
- Face to camera mid-sentence (start cut, no greeting).
- Specific number plus audience: "For Shopify owners: the 10-second fix for abandoned carts."
- Avoid starting with a logo, slow pan, or a blank cover frame.

## 3. Retention & pacing

- Average watch time is one of Mosseri's top three signals; he tells creators to watch "average watch time" in Insights [2].
- Because both absolute and relative watch time count, a tight 45-90 s Reel with high retention can beat a 10 s Reel; but anything past 3 min loses non-follower recommendation eligibility [1][4].
- Cut frequency: no official guidance. Practice: a visual change every 2-4 s; jump cuts in talking heads; on-screen counters for lists.
- Re-hook before the drop-off point visible in Insights retention graphs.
- Loops: seamless end-to-start transitions encourage replays; no official statement that loops are boosted.
- Payoff early enough to earn a send; the share is often triggered at the reveal, so do not bury it in the last second.
- Test hooks with Trial Reels: shown only to non-followers, not on your grid unless you choose to share; reported available to public creator accounts with 1,000+ followers. Reported Mosseri comment: trials usually get less reach than regular Reels (secondary report) [6].

## 4. Captions & text overlays

- Mosseri explicitly called out captions and translations as reach tactics [5]. Use Instagram's native auto-captions where available so text stays editable.
- Sound-off: no current official Instagram figure found. Assume a meaningful share watch muted in feed and Explore; burn in captions or use native captions.
- Font size: no official minimum. Practical: 48-64 px cap height on 1080 px width, two lines max, inside y=270-1250.
- Avoid "mostly text" Reels: Mosseri's 2023 ranking explainer said low-resolution, watermarked, bordered or mostly-text Reels are made less visible (2023, may be dated) [8].
- Caption (post text): first line should be the hook plus keyword, since the rest is folded; keep the keyword natural for search and Google indexing (section 7).

## 5. Audio & sound

- Business accounts lose most of the licensed popular catalog. Instagram Help: Sound Collection has over 14,000 royalty-free songs and sounds safe for Reels and Stories and usable for commercial purposes like ads [7].
- Meta's music guidelines prohibit commercial or non-personal use of the main catalog unless you hold your own licenses; reports say Meta can treat an account as commercial based on activity even if set to "personal" (vendor data, unverified) [7].
- Conflict: vendor blogs say Sound Collection is licensed for Facebook/Instagram only; Meta's help excerpt does not mention this. Assume no reuse on TikTok/YouTube without checking [7].
- Original audio (voiceover, dialogue) is safest for brands and can be reused by others, crediting you.
- Trending sounds help discovery only when available to your account type; do not rip audio from TikTok exports (watermark and licensing issues).

## 6. What the algorithm rewards

Official statements (Mosseri, January 2025 video series) [2][4]:
- Top three signals: watch time, likes, sends. Watch "average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach" in Insights.
- "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." Sends (DM shares) matter most for reaching non-followers, but only "slightly"; blogs that call sends the single dominant signal or claim sends count 3-5x likes overstate it (unverified) [4].
- Comments and saves were not in the top three in these statements; treat them as secondary.
- Originality: since 2022 and expanded in April 2024, Instagram replaces reposted content with the original in recommendations and limits serial aggregators; a 2026 update says watermarks, minor crops or basic reposts do not count as original [3].
- Meta's Transparency Center system cards describe Reels models predicting actions such as watching, liking, sharing; one blog says a Nov 2025 card update lists the probability of watching under 3 s as a key prediction (unverified) [8].
- A Dec 31, 2025 "five signals" memo is cited by one vendor only (unverified) [2].

## 7. Views vs conversion

- Reach: watch time and sends from non-followers. Conversion: profile visits, follows, link taps, DMs.
- Google indexing: from July 2025, public posts from professional accounts (18+) can appear in Google/Bing search; captions, alt text and hashtags are indexable. One source notes Instagram's help center already allowed crawling of public Reels since 2020, so the novelty is disputed [13].
- Write captions with the search phrase a buyer would type ("AI bookkeeping for freelancers"), not keyword stuffing [13].
- DM-keyword CTAs ("comment GUIDE") convert well and also generate comments; deliver via automated DM where allowed.
- Bio: one-line value prop, single link, pinned Reels that explain the product (pinned posts survive the 3:4 grid crop if centered).
- Use Trial Reels to test conversion angles without spamming followers [6].

## 8. Common mistakes & "AI slop" signals

- TikTok watermarks or recycled exports; low-resolution, borders, mostly-text Reels [8].
- Aggregated or reposted content; minor crops or overlays do not make it original [3].
- Generic AI avatar presenter, stock b-roll, default TTS, and template captions; Mosseri (Dec 31, 2025) argued that "authenticity is becoming infinitely reproducible" and that raw, imperfect footage is a temporary signal of reality [14].
- AI labelling: Meta applies an "AI info" label when creators disclose AI use or when it detects C2PA/IPTC metadata from AI tools (even Generative Fill edits can trigger it) [15]. Instagram has been testing a voluntary "AI Creator" profile label (2026) [15]. No confirmed reach penalty for correctly labeled content; claims of 15-80% reach cuts come from an unreliable site [15].
- Going past 3 min and expecting non-follower reach [1].
- Unlicensed music on a business account.

## 9. Quick checklist

- [ ] 1080x1920 MP4, 9:16; cover title centered for the 3:4 grid crop.
- [ ] Length under 3 min for discovery; as short as the idea allows.
- [ ] Text hook on frame 1; payoff stated in the first 3 s.
- [ ] Key text in y=270-1250, x=65-990.
- [ ] Captions on; first caption line = hook + search keyword.
- [ ] One send trigger: something viewers want to DM a specific friend.
- [ ] Check average watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach after 48 h.
- [ ] Business audio: Sound Collection, licensed tracks, or original audio.
- [ ] No watermarks, no reposts; disclose realistic AI content.
- [ ] Test new angles with Trial Reels before posting to followers.
- [ ] Specific CTA (comment keyword / DM) and a one-line bio with one link.
