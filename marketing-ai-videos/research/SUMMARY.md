# SUMMARY: the playbook to read before every video

Condensed from the 12 files in `research/` plus `TASTE.md` (all researched 2026-11-05). Opinionated on purpose. Every figure below comes from those files; vendor-only or contested numbers are marked, and section 8 lists the ones never to quote as fact. Official pages were mostly read through search excerpts, so re-check specs in-tool before a launch.

The standard: super niche, unique, "wow", 4K master, AI voiceover, motion graphics, maximum reach, and conversion to customers.

---

## 1. The 12 rules

1. **Frame one is the ad.** No logo, fade or "hey guys". Because: viewers judge in a fraction of a second, and TikTok counts skips as a negative signal. `(psychology.md, tiktok.md)`
2. **One viewer, one moment, one problem.** Because: a hook in the buyer's own words self-selects buyers, and that is what conversion-optimised delivery rewards. `(psychology.md, marketing-strategy.md)`
3. **Niche creative, broad distribution.** Because: Andromeda and Smart+ let the creative do the targeting, so narrow audiences only shrink the pool. `(paid-ads.md, marketing-strategy.md)`
4. **Novel in one dimension, fluent in all the others.** Because: typicality and novelty together predict liking ("most advanced yet acceptable"). `(psychology.md)`
5. **The visual is the product benefit.** Because: Maybelline's brush coating lashes and Heinz's AI drawing Heinz worked, while Svedka's robots and Volvo's ad with no car did not. `(case-studies.md)`
6. **"Made with AI" is never the idea.** Because: every AI flop in the research led with "first AI..." (Toys"R"Us, Under Armour, McDonald's NL, Svedka). `(case-studies.md)`
7. **Pick concepts that suit what AI does well:** the surreal, comedy, impossible scale. Avoid warmth and a recurring human lead. Because: Kalshi and the FOOH work succeeded, and Coca-Cola and Toys"R"Us drew "soulless". `(case-studies.md)`
8. **Use high-arousal emotion and build in one share moment.** Because: awe, amusement and anger drive sharing, and sends are a top-three Reels signal. `(psychology.md, instagram-reels.md)`
9. **Pay off every loop you open, then end on a peak.** Because: open loops drive resumption (Ovsiankina) more than memory, and the peak and the ending set how the whole video is judged. `(psychology.md)`
10. **Same fingerprints on every new idea.** Because: distinctive brand assets turn varied creative into one brand memory. `(psychology.md, marketing-strategy.md)`
11. **One CTA, with a landing page that repeats it word for word.** Because: two asks split intent, and a message mismatch reads as bait. `(marketing-strategy.md)`
12. **Original only: no reposts, watermarks or templates.** Because: YouTube (inauthentic content), Meta (originality enforcement) and Instagram all demote or demonetise copies. `(youtube-shorts.md, facebook.md, instagram-reels.md)`

## 3. Per-platform checklists

**TikTok**
- [ ] 1080x1920 export at 2,500 kbps or more; key content in roughly y=200-1440, x=60-940
- [ ] Proposition by 3 s; emotional hook by 6 s
- [ ] Keyword spoken and on screen early; caption written as a search snippet
- [ ] A visual change every 2-4 s; re-hook at the midpoint
- [ ] CML or original audio only; the CML licence covers TikTok only
- [ ] Realistic AI content labelled; "comment WORD" CTA plus a pinned comment

**Instagram Reels**
- [ ] Cover title centred for the 3:4 grid; key text in y=270-1250, x=65-990
- [ ] Text hook on frame 1; under 3 min
- [ ] One send trigger ("send this to...")
- [ ] First caption line = hook plus keyword
- [ ] Sound Collection, licensed or original audio; no watermarks
- [ ] Test the angle with Trial Reels; read sends and likes per reach after 48 h

**YouTube Shorts**
- [ ] 9:16, under 60 s if any third-party audio; check Content ID before publishing
- [ ] Topic and payoff in 1-2 s; text inside x=60-880, y=200-1500
- [ ] Title written as the search query
- [ ] Related-video link set to the matching long-form video
- [ ] Judge on engaged views and average % viewed, not public views (replays count since Mar 2025)
- [ ] Check the on-video AI-label overlay doesn't collide with your text

**YouTube long-form**
- [ ] Title and thumbnail promise one outcome, delivered in the first 15 s
- [ ] Intro metric: 50%+ still watching at 30 s
- [ ] Chapters; best moments moved earlier
- [ ] Type legible on a TV (TV has overtaken mobile in the US)
- [ ] Test & Compare 2-3 packages
- [ ] Corrected captions; consider a dubbed audio track
- [ ] Realistic AI disclosed in Studio; UTM CTA in the first description line

**LinkedIn**
- [ ] 4:5, 1080 px wide, under 30 fps
- [ ] Works fully on mute: burned-in captions plus an SRT
- [ ] Relevance by 3 s; one role, one pain, one CTA
- [ ] Post from a person's profile; boost winners as Thought Leader Ads
- [ ] Keep C2PA credentials intact; no engagement bait or pods

**X**
- [ ] Post text = the claim; the video = the proof
- [ ] Motion and the result in frame 1; captions burned in
- [ ] Native upload; link in the first reply
- [ ] Reply to real comments fast
- [ ] Never synthetic news or conflict footage; label realistic AI

**Facebook**
- [ ] 9:16, original or meaningfully transformed footage
- [ ] Person, problem or visual change in 1-3 s
- [ ] Captions; a story arc; short Reels loop cleanly
- [ ] Licensed audio only; no "watch till the end" without a real payoff
- [ ] Meta AI disclosure for realistic video or audio; track with Pixel/CAPI and UTMs

**Paid (Meta, Google, TikTok)**
- [ ] 3-6+ genuinely different concepts, not caption swaps
- [ ] All three orientations supplied (PMax); turn off Advantage+ "Add music" when the ad has its own mix
- [ ] Brand and product within 5 s; one offer, one CTA
- [ ] Report CPA/ROAS and hold rate before CTR
- [ ] Refresh at roughly 2x the baseline cost per result; keep proven winners
- [ ] Toggle TikTok's AI disclosure for realistic AI; election ads need disclosure on all three

## 4. Hook library

| # | Pattern | Example | Principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Result first | "This took 40 seconds. Here's how." | In medias res / attention |
| 2 | Specific question | "Why do 3 of 4 standing desks wobble?" | Curiosity gap |
| 3 | Related interrupt | Creator splashed mid-sentence; the jacket shrugs it off | Pattern interrupt |
| 4 | Held step | "Step 3 is the one nobody does." | Open loop (Ovsiankina) |
| 5 | Contrarian claim | "Stop using templates for cold emails." | Curiosity gap + novelty |
| 6 | Problem callout | "If you still write proposals by hand, watch this." | Specificity / self-selection |
| 7 | Send-bait | "Send this to the friend who still does invoices in Excel." | Social currency |
| 8 | Before/after split | Burnt pan, then peeled clean: "This pan was in the bin yesterday." | Peak-end / fluency |
| 9 | Number + audience | "For Shopify owners: the 10-second fix for abandoned carts." | Practical value |
| 10 | Impossible product at scale | Giant bag driving down a real Paris street | Awe (high arousal) |
| 11 | Shared enemy | "Airlines charge you $40 for this bag. It fits under the seat. Watch." | Righteous anger (high arousal) |
| 12 | Insider detail | Real crag slang, chalked hands on a named route | Identity signalling |
| 13 | Familiar format, odd subject | "Get ready with me", but for a dog | MAYA (fluency + novelty) |
| 14 | Visible counter | "5 tools, 50 seconds" with "3 of 5" on screen | Open loop / fluency |
| 15 | Timely news | "[Vendor] just changed [API]. Here's what it breaks." (only if true) | Triggers (STEPPS) |

Before every brief, pull the three best hooks for the category entry point from the living hook library and write one that beats them. `(marketing-strategy.md)`

## 5. AI production playbook (Nov 2026)

**Which tool for which shot**

| Shot | Tool | Why / watch-out |
|---|---|---|
| Hero shot with sound or dialogue | Veo 3.1 | Native audio, up to 3 reference images, first/last frame, native 9:16; 8 s cap per generation |
| Directed camera move, editing real footage | Runway Gen-4.5, Aleph 2.0 | Strongest editing toolset; Gen-4.5 outputs 720p, so upscale for the 4K master |
| Performance transfer (a real actor's motion) | Runway Act-Two | Up to 30 s; re-run after Aleph edits |
| Longer multi-shot clips, human motion | Kling 3.0 (4.0 GA unconfirmed) | Up to 15 s, shot-by-shot storyboard, Element Library; paid plans only for commercial use |
| Cheap drafts, HDR finishing, restyling a plate | Luma Ray3 / Ray3.14 | Draft Mode, then HiFi up to 4K HDR; licensing pages conflict |
| Social gags, quick concept tests | Pika (H3 Flex, Pikaswaps) | Cheapest; less cinematic; the Free plan has no commercial rights |
| AI voiceover, music, SFX | ElevenLabs v4, Eleven Music, SFX v3 | Direct the read with audio tags; music excludes streaming use |
| Talking head | HeyGen Avatar V | Banned by TASTE.md unless the head *is* the wow; consent recording required |

**Sora is discontinued** (app closed 26 Apr 2026, API shut 24 Sep 2026). Don't build any pipeline around a single vendor; archive prompts and source files locally.

**Prompt formula:** `[Cinematography] + [Subject] + [Action] + [Context] + [Style & Ambiance]` (Google's Veo guide), written one line per slot: shot/lens, subject + action, setting, lighting, ONE camera move, film stock, hex colours, audio (dialogue in quotes, SFX on separate lines). Be physical ("slams", "drags"). Describe what is there, never "no X" (Runway warns negatives can produce the opposite). For image-to-video, prompt only the motion and audio.

**Consistency tricks**
- Approve keyframe stills with the client before spending any video credits.
- Use references: Veo Ingredients, Kling Elements, Aleph keyframes, start/end frames so cuts match.
- One style board and one character sheet; reuse the description string word for word.
- Lock a style bible per client before generating.
- Budget for waste: Kalshi took 300-400 generations for 15 usable clips.

**Kill these AI slop tells:** plastic skin; floaty physics; slow motion on everything; morphing hands, objects or logos (cut at 3-5 s); the default orange-teal grade; centred symmetric framing; uncanny lip-sync; endless drone or dolly moves; everything glossy; montages of unrelated "wow" shots; default narrator cadence; stock "AI business" imagery (TASTE.md bans).

## 6. Strategy in one page

- **Niche creative, broad distribution.** Positioning and creative are narrow; delivery is broad. Each concept owns one category entry point ("launching next month", "our ads are fatigued"), and the algorithm finds the people. `(marketing-strategy.md)`
- **Distinctive brand assets.** Keep a small fixed kit (sonic logo, type treatment, colour, a recurring device, the voice) and vary everything else. Different idea, same fingerprints. Logos and labels are composited, never generated. `(psychology.md, ai-video-tools.md)`
- **Only-ness test.** "We are the only [category] that [benefit] for [audience] in [moment]." If a competitor could sign it unchanged, rewrite it (the same as the TASTE.md logo-swap test).
- **Originality process.** Write the truth and the tension first; collect 20 competitor ads and ban what they share (kill list, AI defaults included); force 3 constrained routes; borrow from two unrelated worlds; score each route before making it.
- **The single-CTA rule.** One action per video and per page; the landing headline and visual repeat the video's promise. Brand-building pieces get a soft CTA only.
- **Budget split.** Start near 60/40 brand/activation for consumer brands and about 50/50 for B2B; only about 5% of B2B buyers are in market at any time (95:5).
- **One idea, many cuts.** Hero plus 5-10 hook variants, 3 aspect ratios, stills, and a "how we made it". Post 3-5 times a week per main channel (Buffer, correlational).

**Metrics per funnel stage** (state the formula each time; compare against your own baseline)

| Stage | Metric | If weak, fix... |
|---|---|---|
| Stop | Hook rate = 3 s views / impressions | The opening |
| Hold | Hold rate = ThruPlays (or 15 s views) / 3 s views | The middle |
| Spread | Share and save rate | The share moment |
| Click | Link CTR | The offer or CTA |
| Convert | CVR, CPA/ROAS | Message match on the landing page |
| Demand | "How did you hear about us?" (free text), branded search | The brand-building content |

Never kill brand content on CTR. Judge it on reach, watch time, shares and branded search.

## 7. Pre-publish quality checklist

**Concept**
- [ ] One named viewer, one moment, one problem
- [ ] Passes the logo-swap test; explainable in one sentence
- [ ] The visuals are the subject, not decoration; a new device, not last video's template
- [ ] A screenshot or share moment that carries the brand fingerprint

**Hook**
- [ ] Frame 1 is the ad: motion, face or product, plus a readable line
- [ ] Payoff or proposition clear by 3 s
- [ ] Every loop you open gets closed; the ending is a resolved peak, not dead air

**Craft**
- [ ] 4K master (3840x2160 / 2160x3840) at high bitrate, plus platform exports (H.264/AAC MP4 at each platform's spec)
- [ ] Loudness around -14 LUFS for social *(the owner's target; it isn't sourced in the research, so confirm per platform)*; music sits well under the voice
- [ ] AI voiceover directed, without the default narrator cadence
- [ ] Captions burned in, typo-checked, inside each platform's safe zone; checked in the in-tool preview
- [ ] Hero shots checked frame by frame: no melting hands, warped text or morphing objects
- [ ] Logo, pack shot, prices and legal lines composited, never generated
- [ ] Grade matches the style bible; no orange-teal default

**Platform fit**
- [ ] Native aspect ratio per platform; no letterboxing, no other apps' watermarks
- [ ] Length within the platform window (Reels <3 min, Shorts <=60 s with third-party audio, X ads <15 s)
- [ ] Search phrase in the title, the on-screen text and the voiceover

**Conversion**
- [ ] One CTA, matching the landing page headline and visual
- [ ] Specific proof: a number, a demo, a named result
- [ ] UTMs set; "How did you hear about us?" field live

**Legal and disclosure**
- [ ] Realistic AI content labelled per platform (TikTok, Meta "AI info", YouTube Studio, X); C2PA/SynthID left intact
- [ ] Music licence covers *this* platform and commercial use (CML, Sound Collection and the YouTube Audio Library don't carry across)
- [ ] Client sign-off, likeness consent (HeyGen consent clip), and credit for every human source and input asset
- [ ] Every claim is substantiated; no invented stats; spec work labelled as spec

## 8. Known-weak evidence (don't quote these as fact)

- TikTok "63% of top ads deliver the message in 3 s" / "71% decide in 3 s": blog-only. `(tiktok.md)`
- A "1.7-second decision window" for Reels: unsourced. The 1.7 s figure is Facebook's 2016 feed average. `(instagram-reels.md, psychology.md)`
- "Sends count 3-5x likes": overstated; Mosseri said "slightly". `(instagram-reels.md)`
- 15-80% reach cuts for AI-labelled content: from an unreliable site. `(instagram-reels.md)`
- Zeigarnik memory advantage: contested by a 2025 meta-analysis. `(psychology.md)`
- "7-15 minutes is the ideal YouTube length" and a "4/5-star satisfaction threshold": no data behind either. `(youtube-long-form.md)`
- Meta "10-20 concepts per ad set", "70% visual difference", "sub-30 s performs best": vendor folklore. `(paid-ads.md, marketing-strategy.md)`
- "1.7x purchase intent with branding in 5 s" and "3x engagement with the logo in 10 s": secondhand, and they conflict. `(paid-ads.md, facebook.md)`
- ABCD "+30% short-term sales, +17% brand": Google-commissioned. `(paid-ads.md)`
- LinkedIn vertical vs horizontal (1.9x vs 2-4x the other way), Thought Leader Ads 2.68% CTR, employee "8x / 561% / 14x": vendor data. `(linkedin.md, marketing-strategy.md)`
- "79% watch with the sound off" (LinkedIn): 2020/21 data, directional only. `(linkedin.md)`
- Maybelline 150M views / +118% sales, Heinz 1.15B impressions: self-reported award entries. Duolingo's 1.7B impressions and Kalshi's "20M viewers": uncorroborated. `(case-studies.md)`
- Refine Labs' 90% attribution gap (vendor, own clients); GEO's "up to 40%" (a lab upper bound); Play Bigger's 76% (a book-era sample). `(marketing-strategy.md)`
- Kling 4K/60fps, Pika H3 Flex speed, HeyGen's Oct 2026 credit rise, and Veo per-second prices: third-party or vendor claims, and prices drift. `(ai-video-tools.md)`
