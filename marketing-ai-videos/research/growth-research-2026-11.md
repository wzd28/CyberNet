# Growth research, November 2026 (organic + paid, CybernetAI and Markable)

Researched 2026-11-08. This file adds to `SUMMARY.md`, `GRANDMASTER.md`, `instagram-reels.md`, `tiktok.md`, `linkedin.md` and `paid-ads.md` and does not repeat them. Those files already cover: Mosseri's Jan 2025 top-three signals (watch time, likes, sends), Trial Reels with auto-share, comment-keyword DM rules (one private reply within 7 days), Andromeda and creative diversity, LinkedIn dwell time and P(skip), the 95:5 rule, Thought Leader Ads vendor CTRs, Spark Ads basics, and the 3-5 posts a week floor.

**How to read the evidence.** Only search-engine excerpts were available in this session. Direct page fetches were blocked for buffer.com, petapixel.com, aol.com, superads.ai, transparency.meta.com, ads.tiktok.com and support.google.com. So a figure here is what the search excerpt says the page says. Tags used:
- **[official]**: the platform's own page or announcement
- **[press]**: a news outlet
- **[vendor]**: an agency or tool blog, often selling something
- **[unverified]**: a single source with no stated method, or sources that conflict

Before quoting any number in public, open the source.

## 1. Instagram (new since the 2026-11-05 files)

**Ranking and policy changes**
- **Hashtags are capped at 5 per post and per Reel** (announced Dec 2025, rolled out gradually). Mosseri: a few specific tags do better than a long generic list, and hashtags help search but "do not increase your reach". [press] https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagrams-implementing-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/ · https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19
  - Action: use 3-5 precise tags such as #scamalert, #dubaiscam, #phishing and #uaesafety. Put the search phrase in the caption, not in tags.
- **Originality is a stated 2026 priority.** Mosseri's year-end memo (31 Dec 2025) lists "improve ranking for originality", plus labelling of AI content and credibility signals about who is posting. [press] https://om.co/2026/01/01/what-is-instagrams-adam-mosseri-really-saying-in-his-year-end-memo/
  - An April 2026 report says Instagram judges originality across what an account posts over about a month, not one post at a time. [press, not opened] https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/
  - The claim that aggregator accounts lost 60-80% of their reach is third-party only. [unverified] https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works
  - Implication: a mostly original feed protects every post. Don't pad the grid with reposted scam screenshots from other accounts unless you add real commentary.
- **"Your Algorithm"** (Reels, US, from 10 Dec 2025) lets viewers see and edit the topics Instagram thinks they like. Instagram said a global English rollout was coming. [press] https://searchengineland.com/instagram-your-algorithm-465987 · https://www.wrdw.com/2025/12/12/what-tech-instagram-rolls-out-new-tool-reels/
  - One vendor says it reached Explore in Apr 2026 and Feed in Jun 2026. [unverified] https://www.socialpilot.co/?p=563048
  - Implication: consistent topic labelling helps. Say "scam", "phishing" and "online safety" explicitly in captions and voiceover so the content lands in a clean topic bucket.
- **Trial Reels**
  - Can now be scheduled (reported Apr 2026). [press] https://www.pulse.bot/creator-economy/news/instagram-allows-creators-to-schedule-trial-reels-d0a7efae-4ca5-4a28-8052-7997a7c2dcb7
  - Instagram's own Jun 2025 figures, reported secondhand: 40% of creators who used Trial Reels posted more often, and Reels reach among non-followers rose 80%. [official via secondary] https://postfa.st/blog/instagram-trial-reels
  - Eligibility claims vary (some guides say 1,000+ followers and a professional account). Check in the app.

**Length**
- Reels can be up to 3 minutes (since Jan 2025). [press] https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-introduces-three-minute-reels-8636683
- Mosseri says there is no perfect length.
- Buffer's own analysis says Reels under 90 s have the best chance of reaching non-followers. [vendor] https://buffer.com/library/instagram-reels-length
  - No Mosseri statement backs that.

**Posting frequency**
- Buffer analysed 2.1M posts from 102K accounts (correlational). 3-5 posts a week more than doubled follower-growth rate compared with 1-2 a week. Reported weekly growth rates by posting frequency:

  | Posts per week | Follower growth |
  |---|---|
  | 1-2 | +0.12% |
  | 3-5 | +0.26% |
  | 6-9 | +0.44% |
  | 10+ | +0.66% |

  - Returns diminish above that range. [vendor, figures from search excerpts] https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/ · https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/study-shows-posting-more-instagram-leads-to-more-reach/757633/
  - Buffer's per-post reach-lift numbers disagree between secondary write-ups. Don't quote them.

**Comment-keyword to DM**
- Allowed through the official API (Private Replies): one reply per comment within 7 days, and more messages only after the user answers. Cold DMs and auto-DMs to new followers are not allowed. As of Apr 2026, mass broadcasts through the API are reportedly unavailable. [vendor; Meta's docs not retrieved] https://creatorflow.so/blog/is-instagram-automation-safe-2026/ · https://www.planoly.com/blog/is-comment-to-dm-allowed-on-instagram.md
- Known failure mode: the trigger fires but no DM arrives because of rate limits. Test before every campaign. [forum] https://community.manychat.com/general-q-a-43/ig-comment-to-dm-trigger-fires-you-messaged-user-name-but-no-dm-is-actually-sent-9114

**Best plan for a small account (synthesis)**
- Post 4-5 Reels a week. Make 2 of them Trial Reels (hook variants).
- Add 1 carousel a week (for example, "5 signs this SMS is fake", built to be saved and sent).
- Use at most 5 hashtags.
- Use a "comment SCAN" keyword only once the DM automation has been tested.

## 2. TikTok

**Search and SEO**
- **Creator Search Insights** shows creators what people search for. It includes a "content gap" filter for topics that are searched often but have few videos. Available in some regions only. [official, Mar 2024] https://newsroom.tiktok.com/creator-search-insights
- Creator Rewards weighs "search value" (already noted in `tiktok.md`).
  - Action: check the in-app tool (Settings > Creator tools, or search "Creator Search Insights") for "scam", "phishing", "fake message", "Etisalat/du SMS", "Emirates Post" and "Dubai Police fine". Make videos for the content-gap topics first.
- Since late Apr 2026, one agency guide says, TikTok shows creators the search keywords assigned to each video and lets them block or suggest terms. [unverified, no TikTok announcement found] https://www.darkroomagency.com/observatory/tiktok-seo
- Guides agree on where the keyword should go: spoken in the audio, on screen, and in the first line of the caption.
  - "Caption keywords beat hashtag keywords" and "audio is indexed" are vendor claims. [vendor] https://metricool.com/tiktok-seo/ · https://blitzcutai.com/blog/tiktok-seo-guide-2026

**Length**
- No primary data shows the algorithm prefers longer videos. Sources agree it rewards retention, not length. [vendor] https://ttcalculator.net/guides/growth/short-vs-long-tiktok-videos/
- Creator Rewards still pays only on videos over 1 minute.
- One source: average video length 38 s in 2026, most-viewed videos about 27 s, highest-earning about 94 s. [unverified, no method] https://www.opus.pro/blog/how-long-should-a-tiktok-be-2026
- Working rule: 20-40 s for reach and "is this a scam?" verdicts. Make 60-90 s explainers only when the story holds attention.

**Posting frequency for a new account**
- "1-4 posts a day" is widely attributed to TikTok's creator materials, but no TikTok page confirming it was found. [unverified] https://nestscale.com/blog/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok-to-grow.html
- Buffer also has an "11M+ posts" TikTok frequency study, but its figures weren't verifiable here. https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/
- Working rule: 1 a day for the first 30-60 days to learn which formats work, then a sustainable 4-5 a week.

**Creative Center**
- The search found no evidence of a trending UAE #scamalert or cybersecurity hashtag. This must be checked by hand in Creative Center > Trends > Hashtags (region UAE, then Saudi Arabia), searching "scam", "fraud" and "cybersecurity". [official tool; no data retrieved] https://www.socialchamp.com/blog/tiktok-creative-center

**Scam-awareness accounts that grew, and what they post**
- **Santander UK "Stop! Scammer Time"** (TikTok's own case study): 3.2B views of UGC under the campaign hashtag, +7.2% ad recall, +2.7% attitude. [official, self-reported] https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/inspiration/santander
  - Format: a branded hashtag challenge plus creators. This is the biggest documented scam-awareness win on TikTok.
- **CNN's TikTok "scam sleuth"** (Jun 2025): over 500K followers. Recurring series "Is it a scam?". Humorous breakdowns of how companies and scammers cheat consumers. The creator's name wasn't in the search excerpts. [press] https://abc17news.com/money/cnn-business-consumer/2025/06/07/tiktoks-scam-sleuth-wants-to-show-you-how-companies-are-cheating-in-a-fun-way/
  - Directly relevant model: one recurring named series, a verdict format, and humour.
- **Malaysia #ThinkTwice (2025-26):** creators (for example Moses Wong) told first-person scam stories. TikTok partnered with police and regulators on a scam-awareness hub. [press] https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/leisure/2026/03/09/thinktwice-storytimes-a-dont-get-scammed-lesson-for-msians · https://worldofbuzz.com/msian-tiktok-creators-turn-personal-stories-into-a-scam-prevention-movement-with-thinktwice-trend/
  - Model: story-time and UGC stitches.
- **Scambaiters** (Kitboga, Jim Browning) are huge on YouTube and Twitch. One tracker puts Jim Browning at about 99% YouTube, so TikTok isn't their main channel. [vendor tracker] https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/jimbrowning/ · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitboga_(streamer)
- **Caution:** a Nigerian creator's fabricated scam story backfired and eroded trust. Never stage fake victim stories. [press] https://www.legit.ng/entertainment/celebrities/1713864-tiktoker-claimed-bandits-demanded-n100m-giveaway-drops-disturbing-fresh-update/
- **Research base:** Google Research analysed 150 TikToks in which creators reported scams. Creator impersonation and verification-badge scams were among six types. Useful for topic ideas. https://research.google/pubs/you-have-been-selected-as-the-winner-characterizing-user-reported-scams-on-tiktok/

**UAE topic hooks with sourced numbers** (each survey defines scams differently, so don't compare them)
- **GASA 2025** (1,000 residents): 7 in 10 had encountered a scam. One scam encounter every 3 days on average. [research org] https://gasa.org/knowledge-base/reports/state-of-scams-in-the-united-arab-emirates-2025
- **Kaspersky** (Apr 2026): 44% faced an online scam in the past year. Half of those were reached through social media. [press] https://securitymea.com/2026/04/14/44-of-uae-residents-faced-an-online-scam-in-the-past-year/
- **UAE Cyber Security Council:** scam messages up 35% in 2025. [press] https://gulfnews.com/uae/uae-cyber-council-scam-messages-rise-35-in-a-year-1.500427804
- **Current scam themes from police warnings:**
  - Abu Dhabi Police, Apr 2026: fake visa and job offers, fake concert tickets, fake insurance. https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/fake-social-media-accounts-visa-concert-ticket-property-scam
  - Dubai Police, Jul 2026: scam ads selling visas and jobs for cash. https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/07/18/dubai-police-issue-warning-over-scam-adverts-offering-visas-and-jobs-for-cash/
- **National campaigns that CybernetAI could align with (not compete with):**
  - Abu Dhabi Police "Be Cautious" (6th edition, Oct 2025). https://gulfnews.com/uae/crime/abu-dhabi-police-launch-sixth-be-cautious-campaign-to-combat-cyber-fraud-1.500317345
  - MoI, Dubai Police and Visa "Don't Talk to Strangers" (Sep 2025). https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/nationwide-fraud-scam-cybercrime-awareness-campaign

## 3. LinkedIn

**What performs (2025-2026 data)**
- **Metricool 2026** (673,658 posts from 63,108 accounts, Jan-Feb 2025 compared with Jan-Feb 2026): [vendor] https://metricool.com/press-release-linkedin-study-2026/ · https://www.socialmediatoday.com/press-release/20260413-metricools-2026-linkedin-study-reveals-the-rise-of-invisible-interactions/
  - Video is now the most-posted format on personal profiles but performs worse than carousels, images and multi-image posts.
  - Carousels earn many times more interactions than single images. Secondary write-ups say 11x or 17x; they disagree, so cite neither.
  - Likes fell 13%, comments 17% and shares 10%, while clicks and other "invisible" interactions rose.
- **AuthoredUp** (about 3M personal posts): document posts get 39% more reach and 30% more engagement than the average post. Video reach is 0.86x the median. [vendor, secondhand] https://www.tryordinal.com/blog/how-often-to-post-on-linkedin
- **LinkedIn itself** (Feb 2025): video watch time up 36% year on year, and video creation growing twice as fast as other formats. [official via press] https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/04/linkedin-amps-up-vertical-video-tools-as-uploads-jump-36
  - Digiday later reported creators seeing video impressions fall. [press] https://digiday.com/media/linkedin-algorithm-tweaks-lead-to-creators-video-views-slump/
- **Net for 2026:** PDF/document carousels are the most reliable organic format. Short native video is for proof and personality, not for reach.

**Founder versus company page**
- The direction is consistent: personal profiles get far more reach.
- The numbers vary a lot and come from vendors:
  - "561% more reach" (Ordinal)
  - company pages reach about 1.6% of followers
  - company page reach down 60-66% between 2024 and 2026
  - [vendor] https://www.agilitypr.com/pr-news/branding-reputation/the-company-page-is-dead-long-live-the-expert/ · https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/linkedin-company-page-or-personal-profile-reach.md
- One dataset (AuthoredUp) shows company pages with a slightly higher median engagement rate. Reach and engagement are different things.
- **Thought Leader Ads:** since Mar 2024, a Page can sponsor any member's post with that member's approval, not only employees' posts. [press] https://searchengineland.com/linkedin-thought-leader-ads-expansion-438409
  - So Markable can boost a client's or organiser's post about its stand. CybernetAI can boost a security creator's post about the product.

**Cadence**
- Buffer (2M+ posts, 94K+ accounts): more posting raises impressions per post. The biggest lift is at 11+ posts a week; 2-5 a week gives a smaller lift. [vendor] https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-linkedin/
- Other studies find reach per post falls at high volume. One claims more than 40% lower median reach at 2+ posts a day. [vendor, unverified] https://theshieldindex.beehiiv.com/p/february-2026-how-posting-frequency-affects-linkedin-reach
- Working rule: 3-4 posts a week from the founder's profile, and 1-2 a week from the company page (mostly reshares and carousels).

**(a) CybernetAI as a security startup**
- Write for the buyer (HR, compliance, finance teams in GCC firms, parents' groups), not for other security engineers.
- Use specific niches ("scams targeting UAE residents"). Original data beats generic tips; CrowdStrike's annual threat report is the model. [vendor] https://gracker.ai/blog/what-are-the-most-effective-cybersecurity-marketing-strategies-for-2025
- Content that has evidence:
  - a monthly "UAE scam of the month" document carousel, anonymised from CybernetAI scans
  - founder commentary on GASA, Kaspersky or Council reports
  - a 30-60 s "I ran this real SMS through the scanner" proof clip
- No measured founder-post benchmarks for security startups were found.

**(b) Markable as an exhibition stand agency** (see section 5)
- Project-showcase posts that credit the client, tag the organiser's page and use the show hashtag.
- Organisers say they react to and reshare posts that tag them. [organiser kits] https://batimat.com/content/dam/sitebuilder/ref/batimat/2024/Guide%20best%20practices%20BAT_EN.pdf.coredownload.228595865.pdf · https://www.thebritishmotorshow.live/exhibitor-news/maximiseroi

**Recommended test structure** (synthesis; percentages are heuristics, not platform rules)
- **Meta learning phase:** about 50 optimisation events per ad set per 7 days. Minimum daily budget ≈ (target CPA × 50) / 7. The 50 is the widely cited Meta figure, but Meta's page wasn't retrieved. [vendor] https://www.savemyleads.com/blog/other/meta-ads-learning-phase
  - Example: a $2 target cost per sign-up needs about $14 a day per ad set.
  - With a small budget, optimise for a frequent event (a free Quick Scan started, or sign-up), not a paid upgrade.
- **Structure**: [vendor consensus] https://segwise.ai/blog/abo-cbo-meta-ads-budget-strategies.md
  - 1 ABO testing campaign: 1 ad set per concept, broad UAE audience, 3-5 genuinely different concepts.
  - 1 Advantage+ / CBO scaling campaign that receives the winners.
  - Changing the budget structure in place resets learning.
- **Suggested split for about $1,000 a month:**
  - Meta about 50%: Partnership or boosted Reels that already won organically.
  - TikTok about 30%: Spark Ads on organic winners. Third-party floors range from $50 a day at campaign level to $100 a day for Smart+ [vendor, unverified], so a small budget suits manual campaigns.
  - Google about 20%: Search only, on exact "is this [X] a scam" and "check suspicious link" terms, plus a branded campaign.
  - Skip YouTube until there's budget.
  - Sources on Spark Ads: https://insense.pro/blog/tiktok-spark-ads · https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-create-spark-ads-for-smart-campaigns-on-tiktok-ads-manager
  - Spark codes: the creator picks a validity period (7/30/60/365 days), and each post needs its own code. One 2026 source says a "Brand Access Portal" replaced codes. [unverified]

**Ad policy constraints for scam and security messaging**
- **Meta**
  - Bans "shocking, sensational or excessively violent" ads. [official text via search] https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/
  - The Personal Attributes policy forbids asserting or implying you know the viewer's attributes. "You/your" phrasing is called out. [official] https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/personal-attributes
  - Deceptive or misleading practices fall under the fraud and scams rules.
  - **My inference, not found in Meta's text:** "You've been hacked", "Your phone is infected" or a fake system alert is likely to be rejected. Use "Got a text like this?" or "Check any link in 5 seconds" instead.
- **Google**
  - Misrepresentation > Unreliable claims forbids claims that present an improbable result as the likely outcome. [official] https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15936857
  - Malicious and unwanted software policies cover rogue security software and products that promise value they don't deliver. [official] https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6020955
  - Avoid "100% scam detection" or "never get scammed again". State what the product does and its limits.
- **TikTok**
  - Rejects exaggerated, unprovable claims and ads that don't match the landing page. [official] https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/guide-ad-policy-for-tiktok-creative · https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-misleading-and-false-content
  - "No fake system notifications or warnings" appears in a third-party summary. [unverified as TikTok wording]
  - Don't use OS-style alert overlays in creative.
- **UAE Media Council Advertiser Permit** (mandatory from 1 Feb 2026): anyone posting promotional content on social media in the UAE, paid or unpaid, needs a permit and must show its number. Brands should contract only licensed creators. Visiting foreign creators need a Visiting Advertiser Permit through a licensed agency. Reported fines conflict (AED 10,000 to "up to AED 1M"). [press/law firm] https://gulfnews.com/uae/new-uae-law-advertiser-permit-now-mandatory-for-influencers-and-creators-for-social-media-1.500427938 · https://www.tamimi.com/law-update-articles/uae-introduces-mandatory-advertiser-permit-for-content-creators
  - **This affects both clients' creator collaborations.** Check at uaemc.gov.ae.

## 5. Exhibition stand agencies (Markable)

**What to post**
- No ranking of agencies by social performance was found, and no stand timelapse with verified views in the millions.
- Evidence-backed content types:
  - **Build timelapse** (empty hall to finished stand; Dubai builds often take about 48 h). This is the format most cited as shareable. Studio52's "AED 25-75K ad-equivalent value" is unsourced. [vendor] https://studio52.tv/blog/?p=36344
  - **Showreels or quarterly highlights** of builds. Plus Exhibition Stands does this. [agency] https://plus-exhibitionstands.com/q2-2024-highlights/
  - **Project showcase crediting the client**, tagging the organiser, using the show hashtag (works on LinkedIn).
  - **Post-show follow-up** that continues show-floor conversations rather than just saying thanks. [vendor] https://www.displaywizard.co.uk/how-to-leverage-social-media-for-trade-show-success
  - **Design-trend pieces**, for example "color drenching" (a 2025 TikTok trend now used in booth design). [vendor] https://www.cortevents.com/blog/color-drenching-your-booth-bringing-tiktoks-viral-hue-saturation-trend-to-life
  - Booths that visitors post about count as free reach, so design a photo moment into client stands. [vendor] https://stackinfluence.com/blog/tiktok-worthy-trade-show-booth-examples-2026
- **Suggested mix (my synthesis):**
  - Instagram/TikTok: timelapse, render-to-reality before/after, event-day walkthrough
  - LinkedIn: the same footage plus a short case note (brief, constraint, result) and a document carousel
  - Tag the client and organiser every time, then boost the client's own post with Thought Leader Ads

**When to post** (lead time)
- Gulfood's process: the contractor submits the design on the organiser portal and receives a Permit to Build. Any change after the permit means a new submission and a new fee. [official, 2024 edition] https://www.gulfood.com/exhibitor
- Vendor timeline: book space 6-12 months out for premium positions at GITEX or Gulfood, and appoint the contractor about 5-6 months out. Design approval about 4-5 months out; technical drawings about 3-4 months out. [vendor] https://adamexpostand.com/exhibition-stand-builder-dubai/
- **So Markable's sales content for show X should peak 6-4 months before X.** Event-day content during X is proof for the *next* edition.
