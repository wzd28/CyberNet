Check Your Social Media Privacy

Answer these questions to see if your social media account is exposing too much information.

Privacy Questions

1. Is your profile private?

2. Do you hide your phone number and email from your profile?

3. Do you turn off location sharing on posts and stories?

4. Do you use two-factor authentication?

5. Do you block strangers from messaging you?

6. Do you review tagged photos before they appear on your profile?

7. Do you avoid using the same password from other accounts?

8. Do you remove suspicious followers or unknown accounts?

Social Media Privacy Tips

  • Keep personal accounts private.
  • Do not show your phone number, email, school, or home address publicly.
  • Turn off location sharing for posts and stories.
  • Use two-factor authentication to stop account takeovers.
  • Limit who can message you or add you to groups.
  • Review tagged photos before they appear on your profile.
  • Use a different password for every important account.
  • Remove fake or suspicious followers.
