10–14. Subscriptions in the app

What you can buy. In the iPhone app you can subscribe to CyberNet AI Pro, monthly or yearly, through the App Store. The price, the length of each period and what Pro includes are shown in the app before you confirm, in the App Store’s own purchase sheet.

Billing. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Apple processes the payment; CyberNet AI never receives your card or bank details. Apple’s own terms for App Store purchases apply as well.

Automatic renewal. A subscription renews automatically at the end of each period, at the price then shown in the App Store, unless you turn off automatic renewal at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.

Managing and cancelling. You manage or cancel the subscription in the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. CyberNet AI cannot cancel an App Store subscription for you. When you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for.

Refunds. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, under Apple’s rules, and you request them from Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com). If Apple refunds a purchase, Pro ends for that purchase.

One account. Pro belongs to the CyberNet AI account you were signed in to when you bought it, so you must be signed in to buy it. It also applies when you use the same account on our website. Restore Purchases in the app links an existing subscription on your Apple Account to the account you are signed in to, if that subscription is not already linked to a different account.

Deleting your account. Deleting your CyberNet AI account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in the Settings app first (your name, then Subscriptions), or Apple keeps charging you until you do.

Plans bought on our website are billed and managed on the website and are described in the website version of these Terms.
