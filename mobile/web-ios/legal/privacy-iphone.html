The CyberNet AI iPhone app

This section applies to the CyberNet AI app for iPhone. The rest of this Policy applies to the app as well, except where this section says otherwise. The app is operated by Marks Events FZ LLE, the data controller named in section 1. The App Store lists the app under the name of its developer, Walid Zeineddine. Privacy questions and requests: support@cybernetai.app.

Checks that stay on your iPhone

Check, Scan QR and the Share sheet run CyberNet AI’s rules on your iPhone. What you check this way is not sent to CyberNet AI or to anyone else, and you do not need an account. The camera is on only while the scanner is open; the picture is read on your iPhone and is not saved or sent. An item you share to CyberNet AI from another app is kept on your iPhone, for at most 24 hours, so the app can offer an AI explanation; it is sent only if you ask for one.

Check, Scan QR and the Share sheet are never sent or logged, also for members of a team you joined on our website. For team members, only Analysis AI and Recovery results are added to the team owner’s activity log (section 3); the app says so before anything is sent for an AI explanation.

AI explanations and Recovery: only with your permission

Analysis AI and Recovery need an account and your permission. Before anything is sent for an AI explanation, the app asks “Allow AI analysis?”. Only if you allow it, and only when you send something yourself, the message, link, screenshot or Recovery description you chose goes from your iPhone to CyberNet AI’s server, which passes it to OpenAI to write the explanation, as described in sections 3 and 6. CyberNet AI records your answer, when you gave it and which version of the notice you saw. You can turn AI analysis off at any time in Account, under Privacy & data; nothing more is then sent for an AI explanation. Explanations you already received stay in your account until you clear your history or delete your account.

A photo or screenshot you attach, or take with the camera, for Analysis AI or Recovery is made smaller on your iPhone and then sent in the same way. The app reads only the picture you choose; it has no access to the rest of your photo library.

Sign in with Apple

If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple gives CyberNet AI your name (the first time only) and your email address or a private relay address that forwards to it. When you delete your account, the app asks you to confirm with Apple so that CyberNet AI can ask Apple to revoke its access.

Stored on your iPhone

Your sign-in session is kept in the iPhone’s Keychain. Your choices (for example, that you have seen the welcome tips) are kept in the app’s own storage. Recovery reminders are scheduled on your iPhone with local notifications: nothing about them is sent to a server, and they never include details of your case. Signing out or deleting your account removes the session and the reminders from the iPhone.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics), only if you allow tracking

The app asks once, with Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether it may track your activity. Only if you choose Allow, the app uses Google Analytics, a service provided by Google, to count which screens of the app are used. Google then receives: the screens you open (Check, Analysis AI, Recovery, Learn, Account), the app version, your device type, your approximate location, which Google works out from your internet connection, and a random identifier that the app creates only for this purpose. Google does not receive anything you check or type, your account details or your email address. Google signals and ad personalisation are off. Google keeps this information for no more than 14 months.

If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, or later turn tracking off in the Settings app under Privacy & Security, then Tracking, Google Analytics is not loaded and nothing is sent to Google; the random identifier is deleted. Every feature works the same either way.

What applies only to the website

The app has no purchases, no advertising, no Google sign-in, no link previews and no cookie bar, and it downloads no code: the code libraries the website loads from jsDelivr are part of the app itself. The parts of this Policy about those features describe the website only. The app does not use your device’s location, contacts or microphone.

Deleting your account in the app

In Account, under Privacy & data, Delete account removes your account as described in section 12. If your account has a paid plan from our website, deleting the account cancels that plan immediately and the rest of the paid period is not refunded; the app shows this before you confirm. Records that our payment provider must keep by law are kept by that provider. The app then removes your session, reminders and shared items from your iPhone.
