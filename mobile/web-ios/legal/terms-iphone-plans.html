10–14. Plans, payments and billing

Plans and limits

The iPhone app offers the same Free, Pro and Business plans as the website. What each plan includes, and its daily limits, are shown on the Plans screen in the app. A plan on your account applies in the app and on the website. Accounts that CyberNet uses for testing and app review may have higher daily limits.

Business teams

A Business plan covers a team of the size you choose. The person who buys it owns the team: they can add and remove members, they see the Analysis AI and Recovery Mode results that members create, and they are responsible for who they add. When the Business plan ends, every member of the team loses Business access at the same time and returns to the plan their own account holds. A member cannot cancel the team’s plan. Checks that run only on your iPhone (Check, Scan QR and the Share sheet) are never shown to a team owner.

Automatic renewal

Pro and Business are auto-renewable subscriptions bought with Apple In-App Purchase. The price and the length of each plan are shown in the app, in your currency, before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Billing by Apple

Apple, not CyberNet, charges you, handles your payment details and sends your receipts. CyberNet does not receive your card number. CyberNet receives signed details of your purchase from Apple (the plan, its dates and its status) so that it can give your account the plan you bought. Apple’s own terms apply to the payment.

Changing or cancelling

You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in the iPhone’s Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose CyberNet AI. You can also open the same screen from Account in the app, with Manage subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the plan stays on until the end of the period you have paid for. You can change between plans in the same place. Restore Purchases, on the Plans screen and in Account, gives a new iPhone the plan you already bought.

Refunds

Purchases made in the app are refunded by Apple, under Apple’s terms, through Apple’s Report a Problem page. CyberNet cannot refund or change a charge made by Apple. Nothing in these Terms limits the rights you have by law.

Deleting your account

Deleting your CyberNet account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it first in Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions, or Apple keeps charging you. The app tells you this before you confirm. A plan that was started outside the app is cancelled when the account is deleted, and a team that you own closes.
