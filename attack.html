What To Do First

  • Do not click more links.
  • Disconnect from Wi-Fi if you think your device is infected.
  • Change passwords from a safe device.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication.
  • Do not pay scammers.
  • Tell your contacts if your account sent fake messages.
  • Back up important files regularly.
