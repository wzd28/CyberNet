General Emergency Cyber Tips

  • Do not panic or keep clicking more links.
  • Change passwords from a safe device.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication.
  • Contact your bank quickly if money or card details are involved.
  • Disconnect from Wi-Fi if your device seems infected.
  • Tell your contacts if your account sent fake messages.
  • Take screenshots as proof before deleting scam messages.
