Check a Website

Enter a website link and answer a few questions. CyberNet will check if the website looks fake or suspicious.

Website Signs

Does the website ask for passwords, OTP, card, or banking details?

Does the website look poorly designed or full of spelling mistakes?

Did you receive this link from SMS, email, or an unknown person?

Does it promise free money, gifts, rewards, or prizes?

Website Safety Tips

  • Open important websites by typing the official address yourself.
  • Do not trust websites sent through random SMS messages.
  • Check spelling carefully in the domain name.
  • Do not enter banking details on unknown pages.
  • Be careful with websites promising free rewards.
