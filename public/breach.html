What To Do If Your Account Was Exposed

  • Change your password immediately.
  • Do not reuse the same password on other websites.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication.
  • Check your email for suspicious login alerts.
  • Log out from unknown devices.
  • Be careful of phishing emails after a breach.
