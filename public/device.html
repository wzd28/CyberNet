Check Your Device Security

Answer these questions to see how protected your phone or laptop is.

Security Questions

1. Do you use a password, PIN, fingerprint, or face unlock?

2. Do you keep your device software updated?

3. Do you use two-factor authentication on important accounts?

4. Do you avoid downloading unknown apps or files?

5. Do you avoid entering passwords on public Wi-Fi?

6. Do you check app permissions?

7. Do you back up important files?

Device Protection Tips

  • Always use a lock screen password or biometric lock.
  • Update your phone, laptop, browser, and apps regularly.
  • Use two-factor authentication for email, banking, and social media.
  • Only download apps from trusted stores.
  • Do not enter passwords on unknown public Wi-Fi networks.
  • Review app permissions like camera, microphone, and location.
  • Back up your important files in case your device is lost or hacked.
