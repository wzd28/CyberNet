Support

Help for the CyberNet AI iPhone app and the website cybernetai.app.

Contact us

Email support@cybernetai.app, or send a message with the form below. We aim to reply within two working days.

Please don't include passwords, one-time codes or card numbers in your message.

Delete your account

In the iPhone app: open Account, then Privacy & data, tap Delete account and type DELETE to confirm.

On the website: sign in at cybernetai.app, open your account, choose the Privacy & data tab, then Delete account.

Deleting your account removes your profile, saved explanations, Recovery Mode cases and settings. If this account has a paid plan from our website, deleting the account cancels it immediately. If you signed in with Apple, the app also disconnects Sign in with Apple from your Apple Account.

If you can't sign in any more, email support@cybernetai.app from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.

Your data

To download a copy of your data, use Export my data in Account, under Privacy & data, in the app or on the website.

Quick Scan, QR scanning and the Share sheet check things on your iPhone. A message, link, screenshot or Recovery Mode description is sent for an AI explanation only after you choose Allow on the "Allow AI analysis?" screen. You can turn this off in Account, under Privacy & data.

Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.

Common questions

CyberNet AI isn't in the share sheet

In the share sheet, scroll the row of apps to the end, tap More, and turn on CyberNet AI.

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your junk or spam folder. In the app, try to sign in again and choose to send a new link.

Is CyberNet AI antivirus software?

No. It is not antivirus software. It helps you judge whether a message, link or QR code looks like a scam, and what to do next.
